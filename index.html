02 · File specs

Preparing your file

Formats
PDF. Not sure your file will work? Send it anyway; we'll check it before printing and follow up if anything needs adjusting.
Resolution
300 DPI or higher for crisp results.
Bleed
If your file includes bleed and cut lines, we'll follow them. If not, we'll confirm the trim before cutting.
Sending your file
Attach your PDF or XML file on the order form. Have a large file or a different format? Email us at orders@pringleprints.ca first and we'll find the best way to send it.

03 · Place an order

Upload your file, choose a finish and delivery method, and pay by card or Interac e-Transfer. Cash is available for pickup and local delivery. Every order gets a confirmation email with a private status link.

04 · What happens next

After you order

We'll check your file before printing, email you if anything needs fixing, and confirm when it will be ready. Printing and hand-cutting take time, so we'll give you a realistic timeline, not a rushed one.

Canada-wide shipping is calculated at checkout. Local pickup and delivery are free in Ottawa–Gatineau.
